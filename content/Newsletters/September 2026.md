I started this month interviewing ACE Scholars and in the fitful throes of a project to help centralize all our data.

# Finds

- **[The Dark Side of Mindfulness](https://archive.is/1H0ZE)\*, by Olga Khazan.** This piece really challenged my priors about meditation -- a practice that I regard(ed) as totally benign, if not for me. I also found it a good account of how unconventional research can rub against religion, business, and the scientific establishment. How do you call into question Buddhist practices, core secular beliefs of meditators, and the multi-billion-dollar wellness industry?
  *\* archive.is link*
- **[Psychedelics Don't Distort Reality — They Reveal How Your Brain Constructs It](https://youtu.be/r5KRnstXYUg?si=8fBsorNwWwelv_di), by Sam Harris and Frame Problems on YouTube**. You don't hear much in the cultural mainstream about psychedelics' history prior to the 1950s. I was also fascinated by the science and philosophy around the mind -- I assumed the idea of *the ego* was mostly a construct and not a tangible structural feature of our minds.
