## Arizona
**Phoenix:** catch a show at The Nash (cheeky self-promotion)
**Tempe**: Casey Moore's Oyster House every day of the week baby!! Consider the Darkside @ Tempe Farmer's Market afterward for a show
**Mesa:** the Mesa Contemporary Arts Museum -- often they have free admission
**Queen Creek**: hashtag brunch at the Olive Mill
**Gilbert:** reserve a table at UnderTow
**Tucson**: clear an afternoon to enjoy the Miniature Time Machine museum
**Oracle**: order the special at The Patio
**Superior**: walk the Boyce Thompson Arboretum when it opens (to increase your chances of coatimundi)
**Flagstaff:** buy something pickled at the Flagstaff community farmer's market; if off-season, go get slizzered at the Monte Vista cocktail lounge
**Sedona:** visit the Chapel of the Holy Cross (bring patience for parking)
**Payson:** hike water wheel fall or bring a propane fire pit to cook lunch on the Rim
**Pine**: eat an elk dog at Elky's
**Prescott**: whiskey row, but on a weekend only

## California
**San Diego**: gyros from Cojack's and close out Mission Beach
**Coronado**: order something at the deli at Boney's and enjoy it on the beach; whichever end of the island you go to, be sure to walk
**Klamath**: walk Trees of Mystery

## Ohio
**Cleveland:** West Side Farmer's Market, lunch at TownHall up the street, then step into Mitchell's, the ice cream factory
**Akron**: find the nearest open antique mall >40,000sqft
**Barberton**: why are you here? go to Durbin Magic Freeze

## Colorado
**Aurora:** bike the tollgate trail between a sandwich shop and Cherry Creek Reservoir and enjoy lunch
**Denver**: For public transit points — Take the rail to Union Station and walk the canal to the REI flagship store (only if the weather is nice). For something that could take you all day indoors — the Denver Art Museum.
**Golden**: bike or walk down Clear Creek at sunset (to understand why it's called Golden)
**Evergreen**: hike Maxwell Falls, then enjoy a mountain pie at Beau Jo's

## New Mexico
**Santa Fe:** the original Meow Wolf, of course
**Albuquerque:** dinner at M'tucci's on a hot evening
